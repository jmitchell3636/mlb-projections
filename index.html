Player Projections

Five-year projections for every MLB hitter and pitcher, fit through the 2026 season. Component rates use fitted regression and season weights, per-horizon LightGBM and neural-network corrections, and a Statcast layer: an xwOBA-minus-wOBA luck correction for hitters, and a skill layer (velocity, whiffs, called strikes and more) for pitchers. Projected playing time includes injury and role risk; full-season values show a healthy year. Every number carries an 80% range.

About these numbers

Hits, doubles, triples, walks and strikeouts come straight from the projected rates. R, RBI and SB (hitters) and W and L (pitchers) are estimates: they depend on lineup slot, teammates and run support, so treat them as rougher than the rest. Pitcher hits allowed and WHIP assume league-average luck on balls in play. Players with no major-league time yet (most prospects) are not projected.

Roles come from each pitcher's latest season: closer = 10+ saves, setup = 3+ saves or 10+ holds. Saves are projected for 2027 only and are the least certain number here, since closer jobs change hands. Leverage WAR scales WAR by (1 + leverage) / 2, using typical leverage by role (closer 1.8, setup 1.35, middle 0.95), an approximation of how FanGraphs credits relievers for pitching in high-leverage spots.

How the standings work

Team wins add up the projected WAR of each roster: hitters capped at 6,150 PA and pitchers at 1,450 IP per team, with gaps filled at replacement level. Rosters are each player's latest team at the end of 2026, so offseason trades and free-agent signings aren't reflected yet. Odds come from 20,000 simulated seasons using the method's actual error in a 2008–2025 backtest (9.9 wins, against 10.2 for last year's record regressed halfway to .500), which covers both roster changes and luck. Range is the middle 80% of simulated win totals; Div and Playoff are the share of simulations in which the team wins its division or makes the playoffs. Playoff odds assume three division winners and three wild cards per league.

Validation: refit at every season from 2006 using only data available at the time, scored 1–3 years ahead against Tom Tango's classic Marcel, with a locked holdout season scored once. RMSE of wOBA (hitters) and FIP (pitchers), weighted by playing time. Fielding includes positional value and, from 2016, Statcast Outs Above Average.
